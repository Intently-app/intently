This policy covers the Intently website at **liveintently.app** only. Each app has its own policy

## What we collect

- **Contact form:** your name, email address, the app you're asking about and your message. We
  use them only to reply, and they're delivered to our inbox through **Resend**, an email
  provider.
- **Server logs:** the site runs on **Cloudflare**, which processes your IP address and basic
  request details to serve and protect the site.
- **Visit statistics:** see below.

The site shows no ads, runs no advertising trackers and doesn't sell data. What you choose or type
on the chapter pages never leaves the page.

## Visit statistics

**Google Analytics 4** measures which pages are read, how visitors arrive (search, links,
campaign tags), the device type, browser and rough location (country and city, from the IP
address, which Google doesn't store). It also counts clicks on the links to the Respite website
and stores, with the page and the part of the page they were on. It uses the same measurement as
the [Respite website](https://respite.liveintently.app/privacy/website/), so a visit that goes on
from here to that site counts as one visit.

- **In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland** it runs in Google's Consent Mode with analytics storage
  denied. It sets no cookies, keeps no identifier between pages or visits, and sends only
  cookieless pings that Google uses for aggregate estimates.
- **Elsewhere** it sets first-party cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) on liveintently.app that last up to
  2 years. These cookies recognise a returning browser, so that visits and visitors can be counted.
- **Everywhere**, advertising storage, ad personalisation and Google signals are off. Nothing is
  used for advertising or linked to a Google account.

Google processes this data as our processor; see [how Google uses data from sites that use its
services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). To opt out of Google Analytics
in any browser, use Google's [opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or block
cookies or scripts for this site in your browser. The site works the same without them.

## Retention

Messages stay in our inbox for as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete them.
Cloudflare keeps request logs according to its own retention policy.

## Your rights

Email [support@liveintently.app](mailto:support@liveintently.app) to access or delete what you
sent us. Changes to this policy are posted here with a new date.
