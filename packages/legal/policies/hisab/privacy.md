Hisab is a ledger you fill in by talking to an AI app, by letting your bank's and wallet's text messages
log payments on Android, or by adding receipts and statements.

**Developer:** MD Sadakat Hussain Fahad, publishing as Intently ·
**Contact:** [support@liveintently.app](mailto:support@liveintently.app)

Hisab is in private testing. This policy covers the website and waitlist, and the app as it works today.

## The short version

- We keep what you put in your ledger so we can show it back to you.
- We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
- Hisab can't move money and never asks for your bank login.
- On Android, the text of your bank messages stays on your phone.
- If you connect your own AI app, its maker's terms apply to what it sees.

## If you only join the waitlist

We store your email address, the date you joined and the page you joined from. We use it to send you
one email when Hisab opens. We don't store your IP address with it. It's kept on Cloudflare.

To be removed, email [support@liveintently.app](mailto:support@liveintently.app) from that address.

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

## If you use Hisab

### Your account

When you sign in with Google, we receive and keep your email address, your name and your Google
account's identifier. We keep sign-in sessions so you stay signed in.

### Your ledger

We store what you record: accounts, transactions, amounts, dates, payees, notes and the reports you
save. Every change is written to a log with who made it, whether that was you or a connected AI, along
with the values before and after. That log is how undo and the activity list work.

### Receipts, statements and forwarded email

Files you add are stored so they can stay attached to your entries. If you turn on email forwarding,
mail sent to your ledger's address is read to create draft entries, and its attachments are stored.

### Bank and wallet messages on Android

Reading messages is off until you turn it on, and Android asks for your permission first. Hisab reads
messages on your phone and looks only for ones that match a known bank or wallet format. The text of a
message is never sent to us. For a message that matches, the phone sends the amount, currency, date,
payee or description, and a fingerprint (a one-way hash) so the same message isn't counted twice.
One-time codes and personal messages aren't used.

You can turn this off in the app, or remove the permission in Android settings, at any time.

### AI apps you connect

If you connect an AI app such as Claude, it can use Hisab with the permissions you approve, on the
ledgers you choose. What it reads becomes part of your conversation with that app and is handled under
that company's terms. You can see what a connection has done, and end it, in Hisab's settings.

### Hisab's built-in AI

When you type or say a line in Hisab, or add a receipt or PDF statement, we send that text or file to
an AI provider, along with your ledger's account names, base currency and today's date, so it can
understand what you mean. Nothing it reads or writes is kept in our logs.

## Who handles data for us

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, databases, file storage and the waitlist.
- **Google:** sign-in.
- **Anthropic:** Hisab's built-in AI once it opens to the public.
- **Z.ai:** Hisab's built-in AI during private testing only.

Exchange rates are fetched from public sources, and those requests contain none of your data.

## How it's protected

Each ledger is kept in its own separate database, and every request is checked against who belongs to
that ledger. Connections use HTTPS, and Cloudflare encrypts stored data. Amounts, payees, notes and
message text are kept out of our logs. On your phone, you can put Hisab behind your fingerprint, face
or PIN.

## How long it's kept, and how to delete it

Your ledger is kept for as long as you have an account. A proposed AI change that nobody approves
expires after 7 days.

You can download a copy of any ledger, and delete your account, in Hisab's settings on the web or from
the menu in the Android app. [Deleting your account](/delete-account/) erases your account, every ledger
you own and its files from our live systems straight away, and it can't be undone. Our hosting provider's
automatic recovery history can hold a copy for up to 30 days before it ages out, and it's only used to
recover from a failure. The download lists your receipts but doesn't
include the files, so email us if you need those.

If you can't sign in, email [support@liveintently.app](mailto:support@liveintently.app) from your
account's address and we'll finish within 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete your personal data, and you can object to how we
use it. Depending on where you live, you may also be able to complain to your data protection
authority.

## Children

Hisab isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell people who have an
account before it takes effect.
