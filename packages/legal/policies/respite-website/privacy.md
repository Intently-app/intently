This policy covers the Respite website at **respite.liveintently.app** only. The apps have their

## The short version

- The site shows no ads and doesn't sell data.
- We measure visits with Ahrefs Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, and Google Analytics.
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics sets no cookies either.
- The contact form and the account deletion page send only what you type into them.

## Visit statistics

**Ahrefs Web Analytics** counts page views, referrers and clicks on store buttons. It sets no
cookies and doesn't identify you.

**Google Analytics 4** measures which pages are read, how visitors arrive (search, links,
campaign tags), the device type, browser, rough location (country and city, from the IP address,
which Google doesn't store) and clicks on App Store, Google Play and Chrome Web Store buttons.

- **In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland** it runs in Google's Consent Mode with analytics storage
  denied. It sets no cookies, keeps no identifier between pages or visits, and sends only
  cookieless pings that Google uses for aggregate estimates.
- **Elsewhere** it sets first-party cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) that last up to 2 years. These cookies
  recognise a returning browser, so that visits and visitors can be counted.
- **Everywhere**, advertising storage, ad personalisation and Google signals are off. Nothing is
  used for advertising or linked to a Google account.

Google processes this data as our processor; see [how Google uses data from sites that use its
services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). To opt out of Google Analytics
in any browser, use Google's [opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or block
cookies or scripts for this site in your browser. The site works the same without them.

**Store links:** when you click a link to an app store, the site adds campaign tags (such as
`utm_source`) to say which page sent you, so the store can count installs by source. The tags you
arrived with are kept in your browser's session storage until you close the tab.

**Reading on the blog:** your text size and how far you have read each article (so the blog can
offer "Continue reading" and mark what you have read) are kept in your browser's local storage.
They never leave your device; clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

## Forms

- **Contact form:** your name, email address, subject and message are emailed to us through
  Cloudflare Email Routing so we can reply. They aren't stored on the website.
- **Delete your account:** the email address you enter, and a reason if you give one, go to the Respite server to send the
  confirmation link. The [app policies](/privacy/) describe what deletion removes.

## Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare handles your IP address and the
pages you request in order to serve them and protect the site from abuse.
