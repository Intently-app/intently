Every app has a ceiling: what its makers thought of. Respite lets you set limits, pauses and schedules. But your life is not a settings screen, and some of what would help you most, such as "why do my Tuesdays fall apart?" or "write me a pause message that actually works on me", is not something we should be hard-coding. So we are opening the door to your own AI.

## What is bring your own AI?

Respite now speaks **MCP** (the Model Context Protocol), an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to apps. With a Respite Plus account you can connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another MCP-capable assistant to Respite, sign in, choose what it can do, and let it work with your Respite data for you.

The idea is simple: **your app is no longer limited by what we built.** Your AI does the thinking. It already knows how you write, what you are working on and how you like to be talked to. Respite supplies the data and the levers.

## What works today, and what is coming?

This is a staged rollout, and we want to be straight about where it is.

| | Status |
|---|---|
| Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor and sign in | Works today |
| Check your account: plan, devices, when connected | Works today |
| Read your usage and insights | Planned next |
| Manage limits, schedules and goals | Planned |
| Write your own pause messages | Planned |
| Custom if-this-then-that rules | Planned |

Only the first two exist today. Everything else in this post is what we are building toward, and we will not describe a tool as available until it is. The [What's new](/whats-new/) page will say when each one arrives.

## How does it work, and what about privacy?

- **We do not run an AI on your data.** Respite has no AI of its own here and pays for none. The model is the one you chose, on the plan you already have.
- **Your data only goes to the AI you connect,** for the permissions you allow. There is no other recipient.
- **You choose the permissions.** Access is split into separate permissions: reading usage and history, managing limits and schedules, managing the messages that appear at the pause, and managing custom rules. The consent screen shows what the assistant is asking for, and you can allow or deny it.
- **You can revoke access any time** by disconnecting Respite in your AI app. Respite also keeps a list of connected AIs for your account and lets them be removed.
- **Sign in is the same as the app:** Google or Apple.

Our broader approach to data is in [privacy-first screen time tracking](/blog/privacy-first-screen-time-tracking/).

## Why connect an AI to a screen time app?

A few things people can already do with a general assistant and their own numbers, which the connection will make easier:

- Spot patterns, such as which days and times your use spikes.
- Turn a vague goal into limits that fit your life.
- Draft honest, personal messages for the moment you reach for an app.

Our practical guide, [how to use ChatGPT or Claude to reduce screen time](/blog/use-chatgpt-claude-to-reduce-screen-time/), shows prompts you can use right now, with or without Respite connected.

## What does it cost?

Bring your own AI is part of [Respite Plus](/blog/respite-plus-whats-included/). Respite stays free for everyone. Whatever your AI assistant costs, you pay it directly.

## How do I connect?

Step-by-step instructions for each assistant are in [how to connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor to Respite](/blog/connect-claude-chatgpt-cursor-to-respite-mcp/). The short version: add Respite as a custom connector with the address `https://respite-api.liveintently.app/mcp`, sign in, and allow.
