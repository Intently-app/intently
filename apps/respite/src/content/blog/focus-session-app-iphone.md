Sometimes you do not need a rule for the whole week; you need the next hour. You have a report to finish or a chapter to read, and the phone is in reach. A focus session is a block you start on purpose: pick a length, pick the apps, and they stay closed until it is over. Here is how it works in Respite on iPhone, what is free, and what Plus adds.

## How does a focus session work in Respite?

1. **Add your apps.** A session blocks apps you already track. Add the ones that pull you away on Home.
2. **Tap Focus** on the Home tab. The sheet opens with the title "Focus".
3. **Choose how long.** The choices are 15, 25, 45, 60 and 90 minutes.
4. **Choose what to block.** The default is everything you track, or pick only the ones you want closed.
5. **Tap Start.** Your apps stay closed until the time is up. You can end it early.

On iPhone this uses Apple's Screen Time, so the apps you pick stay private on your device, and the block holds even if you leave Respite.

The cap is 25 minutes on the free plan. If you tap a longer length, Respite shows the plans. Everything else about a session is the same. See [Respite Plus: everything that is included](/blog/respite-plus-whats-included/) for the rest of Plus.

## What is the Live Activity?

A Live Activity is a small panel that iOS keeps on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while something is happening. For a Respite focus session it shows the time left and an **End** button. It is useful for two reasons: you can see how long is left without opening anything, and you do not have to unlock your phone, and walk past its apps, to end a session you set by mistake.

Two honest notes. Starting a Live Activity needs Respite in the foreground, so a session started some other way may run without one. And if Live Activities are switched off in Settings, the session still runs. It only means no panel.

## What does the Control Center control do?

With Plus, you can add the Respite control to Control Center. One tap starts a 25-minute session, and tapping it again while a session runs ends it. It is the fastest way to start: swipe down, tap, and your apps are closed. Without Plus the control opens the plans screen instead.

## Focus session or schedule?

They are different tools:

- **Focus session:** a one-off block you start now for 15 to 90 minutes. Good for deep work, study and "I need to finish this".
- **Schedule:** repeats on chosen days and hours, and a pass or snooze does not lift it. Good for bedtime and work hours. See [how to schedule app blocking](/blog/schedule-app-blocking-iphone-android/).

Many people use both: a Bedtime schedule that runs every night and focus sessions when the work demands it.

## What if I just break the session?

You can end a session early, because Respite is designed to give you the choice and not to lock you in. That matters: a block you cannot escape tends to make people either resent the app or uninstall it. If you find yourself ending sessions the moment they start, the length is probably too long, so start at 15 or 25 minutes. For a firmer measure, a schedule is locked while it runs. ## Tips for better focus sessions

- **Put the phone out of reach as well.** A session blocks apps, not temptation.
- **Start before you feel ready.** The Control Center control makes starting a one-second decision.
- **Block everything for deep work; block a few for lighter tasks.**
- **Pair it with a break.** 25 minutes of focus and five off is a classic rhythm, and 25 is exactly the free length.
- **Look at your streak, not your phone.** Staying within limits on a day you also did a session is a good way to earn acorns; see [how acorns work](/blog/how-acorns-streaks-streak-freezes-work/).
