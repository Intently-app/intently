Respite's game layer is small on purpose, but it has a few rules worth knowing. This page is the reference: every number below comes from the app's game rules, so it is what you will actually see. If you want the why before the how, start with [introducing acorns, quests and leagues](/blog/introducing-acorns-quests-and-leagues/).

## How do I earn acorns in Respite?

| You do this | You get |
|---|---|
| Keep a day within all your limits | 20 acorns |
| Walk away at the pause | 2 acorns each, up to 10 a day |
| Finish a daily quest | 10 to 30 acorns |
| Reach a 3-day streak | 25 acorns |
| Reach a 7-day streak | 50 acorns |
| Reach a 14-day streak | 100 acorns |
| Reach a 30-day streak | 200 acorns |
| Reach a 100-day streak | 500 acorns |
| Open the weekly chest after 4 or more kept days | 30 to 80 acorns |
| Accept a friend's invite (both of you) | 50 acorns |
| Place in the top three of a weekly league | 100, 60 or 40 acorns |

Acorns are also **XP**. Every acorn you earn counts toward your level, and spending acorns does not take XP away.

## What counts as a kept day?

A day is kept when your total time on each app or site you have a limit for stays within that limit. Three details matter:

- **It is combined across devices.** If you have set a limit on an app or site and use it on two devices, both count toward one total.
- **You need a limit.** With no limits set there is nothing to keep, so no day is counted. Set one in the app, then see [how to reduce screen time](/blog/how-to-reduce-screen-time/) for picking a sensible number.
- **It settles when the day closes.** Today's acorns arrive after midnight, once there is a full picture. Respite looks back over the last week when it settles, so a day you missed opening the app is still paid.

## What is a walk-away?

At the pause, you can leave instead of continuing. Each time you do, that is a walk-away, worth 2 acorns. The cap is 10 acorns a day (five walk-aways), so it rewards the habit without turning into a farm. Walk-aways also feed one of the daily quests.

## How do daily quests work?

You get three quests a day, picked for you from these types:

| Quest | Reward |
|---|---|
| Walk away 3 times | 10 acorns |
| Walk away 5 times | 20 acorns |
| Open your insights | 10 acorns |
| Stay under 75% of one app's daily limit (at least 5 minutes) | 20 acorns |
| Keep all your limits today | 30 acorns |

The two limit-based quests only appear once you have a limit, and they complete only when the day ends, since only the end of the day proves you stayed under. Quests from the last two days stay claimable.

## How does the streak work?

Your streak is the number of days in a row you have kept, counting back from yesterday. Today joins it once today closes. Milestone bonuses pay once per streak, at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days. A day that is not kept ends the streak unless a freeze covers it.

## How do streak freezes work?

A freeze is a safety net for a day that went over.

1. **Go over a limit.** When an app is over its limit today and you have a freeze, the Journey tab offers "Use a freeze on" that app.
2. **Tap it.** That app no longer breaks today.
3. **Cover a recent day if you need to.** The game accepts a freeze for today or any of the last three days.

A few rules:

- A freeze covers one app for one day. If two apps went over, you need two freezes.
- A frozen day keeps your streak going but pays no acorns for that day.
- You cannot freeze a day that was already kept.
- You get **3 free freezes each month**. More cost 150 acorns each, and you can buy as many as you like.

## What is the weekly chest?

Keep at least four days in a week and the chest opens on Sunday for 30 to 80 acorns, a number that is set for you for that week. You can claim it from Sunday onward.

## How do levels work?

Level 2 starts at 100 XP, level 3 at 250, level 4 at 450 and level 5 at 700. After that each level asks a little more than the last: 300 XP for level 6, then 350, 400 and so on. Levels are for bragging rights and for your Biscuit; nothing is locked behind them.

Two app themes are listed in the shop but are not on sale yet. Outfits can be worn by Biscuit and, in leagues, appear next to your name.

## Tips for getting more from the game

- Use a free freeze on the day you know will be rough. It is there to be used.
- Check quests in the morning; the two limit quests are the ones worth planning around.
- Do not set limits so tight you hate them. A kept day is worth the same 20 acorns whether the limit was generous or strict. See [gamified screen time apps](/blog/gamified-screen-time-apps/) for why modest, achievable limits work best.
- Join a league only if it motivates you. Details are in [Respite leagues and friends](/blog/respite-leagues-and-friends/).
