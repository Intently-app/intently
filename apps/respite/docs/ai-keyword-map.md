## Reasons some keywords are not targeted with new content

- **Head terms owned by authority or vendor sites** (phone addiction, phone addiction symptoms / treatment / help,
  best app blocker reddit, how to reduce screen time reddit, how to stop doomscrolling): mapped to the closest existing
  page for completeness; no new page, no extra effort. Reddit queries are a participation task, not a site task.
- **iOS 27 Screen Time** (ios 27 screen time changes, ios screen time not working): the research has only
  secondary sources (MacRumors, 9to5Mac, TechRadar via search summaries), nothing verified on Apple's own pages. No
  dedicated post until a primary source is read; `apple-screen-time-alternatives` carries the query meanwhile.
- **Desktop blockers** (app blocker for pc / mac / windows) and **claude desktop mcp setup**: high competition, off
  product; secondary mentions only.
- **Google Digital Wellbeing AI features**: not covered by the research, so no claim is made.

## Conflict rules

- "Connect X to screen time" tutorials: one page per assistant (Claude, ChatGPT); the generic connect post keeps
  Cursor, other clients, permissions and troubleshooting. Do not duplicate step lists; link instead.
- "Best AI app blockers" (roundup) vs "AI screen time coach apps compared" (in-app coach apps and alternatives to
  them): the roundup covers all five AI patterns in one table; the coach page goes deep on the in-app negotiator type.
- `/ai/` is the single hub; every AI post links to it, and it links to each AI post once.
