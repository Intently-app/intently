Usage and crash data

Respite sends anonymous counts, such as which screens are used and how pauses end, and crash reports. Well-known apps such as Instagram or YouTube are named; any other app only by its type, such as game. Your email and what you do in other apps are never sent. Turn this off and nothing more is sent.
