# mind family recipe

Reference images: `psychology-of-app-addiction.svg` (object on a stage) and `science-of-friction.svg` (landscape).

- **Light source = a layered paper sun/halo.** Use 3–4 concentric circles stepping from `#DDEDF3` through `#E5F1F6` and `#EDF6F9` to `#F7FBFC`/white. Each circle gets the `sh` drop shadow, and a white radial `glow` sits on top. Put it behind the subject as a halo, or in the upper right as a sun. It is always pale and never warm.
- **Sky:** a vertical gradient between `#DCEEF4` and `#F7FBFC`. Leave the top third mostly empty as negative space. No clouds or birds, because they read as clip-art.
- **Ground = 4–6 stacked wavy layers, every one with `filter="url(#sh)"`** (dy 6, std 8, .18). Far layers are pale mist (`#C9E2EB`, `#A9CCD9`). Near layers darken toward `#2F6079`/`#24465A`. The foreground strip at the very bottom is always the darkest. Draw curves with `C`/`S`, keep crests gentle, and let neighbouring layers overlap so no vertical seams show.
- **Greens are sea-glass sage, not grass.** Use `#B8D6D0`, `#8DB7AE`, `#79A79E`, `#64958C`, `#467872` and `#2B5560`. They sit next to the mist blues without turning the image green. Use the true `#3E9B4F` only as one or two tiny spots.
- **Subject:** one central object or place in x 560–1040. It is built from flat paper pieces (bezel, glass, panel, rim), and each piece gets its own `sh` shadow. Small parts (balls, foam, leaves) use the tighter `sh2` shadow (dy 3, std 3, .2–.22). Highlights are small white ellipses at 45–60% opacity, upper left, to match the light.
- **One warm accent, about 60–75 px, at the point of the story** (the dispensed gumball, the leaf resting on the pool). Fill it with a radial or linear gradient from `#F3BC45`/`#F6C25A` to `#E8A317` to `#B97F0C`. Behind it goes a soft `#FCEBC4` halo or ellipse so it pops at thumbnail size. Nothing else in the image is warm.
- **Small details:** 2–4 lollipop paper trees (thin trunk plus an ellipse canopy) can set the scale. Water is drawn as a `#BDE1EC`→`#8FBFD2` ribbon with 3 white streaks, plus foam ellipses where it lands.
- **Workflow:** check the 800px preview. If the subject can't be read as a silhouette, push the contrast between it and the halo behind it. Shadows only work on separate shapes, so split a shape into layers rather than adding strokes. Expect 7–12 KB of SVG and a JPG under 120 KB.
