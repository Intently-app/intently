# Product family recipe

Reference images: `svg/introducing-acorns-quests-and-leagues.svg`, `svg/how-acorns-streaks-streak-freezes-work.svg`. Copy their `<defs>` as a starting kit.

- **Backdrop**: one radial gradient, centred at (800,360), r 980 in user space: `#E4F2F7` → `#C9E5EF` (0.4) → `#A6CCDC` (0.8) → `#7FAEC3`, plus a blurred `#5E8FA6` ellipse (opacity 0.25) along the bottom edge to darken the floor. Keep the centre a little darker than white so that white or cream light (rays, halo) still shows up behind the subject.
- **Stage disc**: every hero sits on the same mist plinth. The top is an ellipse at (800,735) with rx 580 and ry 108, filled with `#EEF5F8` → `#BDE1EC`. Under it is a 30px side band with a horizontal `#8DB8CB` → `#5E8FA6` → `#2F6079` gradient, a 3px `#F7FBFC` rim stroke, and a blurred ink shadow (ellipse at y 812, opacity 0.22). The subject stands on it with its base around y 700–785.
- **Light**: the key light is upper-left. Lit faces get the light end of each gradient and a soft white or cream highlight stroke on their left or top edge. Faces on the right are about 25% darker. The contact shadow under each object is a blurred `#24465A` ellipse at 0.32–0.4 opacity, nudged slightly to the right, with a tighter, darker ellipse right at the base.
- **Acorn**: reuse `<g id="acorn">` exactly as it is, scaled 0.8–1.1 with `<use href>` (internal use is fine). The nut is `#EBBC85` → `#C98B4E` → `#8A5A2B`, with a cream highlight sliver on the left. The cap is `#A9764A` → `#4E2F15` with two rows of scallop strokes, a tan rim highlight and a curled stem. Every loose acorn on the disc gets its own small blurred shadow (rx 30–36, ry 7–8).
- **Warm focal**: one orange object or light carries the story (the chest, the flame), built from `#F4B53A` / `#E8A317` / `#B97F0C`, with cream `#FCEBC4` / `#FFF7E2` glows. Never put translucent white or cream overlays on top of the focal orange, because that turns it olive. Draw glass, ice and glare under it, or keep them thin.
- **Secondary colours**: navy `#24465A` / `#16303F` works for structural trim such as straps, rims and poles, with rivets in `#BDE1EC`. Green `#3E9B4F` / `#7CC489` / `#D6EFD9` is for small living accents: pennants, leaves, sprouts, ribbons. Brown is reserved for acorns and wood.
- **Atmosphere**: the energy layer goes last, just before the grain. A launch or celebration gets radial-faded light rays and leaf confetti with a midrib line (the distant leaves blurred 2px). A calm how-to gets a white halo ellipse behind the subject plus a few `#spark` 4-point stars or `#flake` snowflakes. Keep it to 6–12 pieces, scattered in an arc around the subject and never across it.
- **Build**: use oblique 3/4 boxes with a depth offset of about (+75..110, −45..60). Round the corners (rx 14–30, or round-joined strokes) so objects read as toys. Keep the focal subject inside x 420–1180 and y 150–790. Expect about 14–18 KB of SVG and a JPG of about 100–120 KB.
- **Staging variety**: don't reuse the round stage for every image. The 18 heroes use six stagings: round stage (chest, ice cube); floating iso tile or diorama (console, scanner, leagues, MCP house); square plinth (glasses); tabletop with a wall horizon (recipe box, cloche, cake, metronomes, snow globe at night); close-up crop (ratchet panel, map); and objects floating on a colour field (tin-can platforms, adapter, key ring, sundial island). Iso work uses true 30° projection, with plane matrices `matrix(cos30,±0.5,0,1,…)` for vertical faces and `matrix(cos30,0.5,−cos30,0.5,…)` for the floor.
- **Hard-rule traps found**: two round holes above a slot read as a face, so keep socket and port holes in a single row or use one slot. A large round dark port reads as a camera or eye.
