# Guide family recipe (from the pilots: stop-tiktok-scrolling, stop-checking-phone-morning)

- **Sky first.** The top ~55% is a graded "sky" (a real sky or a back wall used like one): `#5E8FA6`/`#7FAABD` at the top, through `#A9D0DF`/`#BDE1EC`, to `#DCEEF4`/`#F7FBFC` at the horizon. Add one large soft radial glow (paper white, or `#FCEBC4` when the light is warm) around the light source. Leave most of the sky empty. Use at most two or three thin lens-shaped clouds or blurred light shafts, never cartoon puff clouds.
- **One horizon line** at y≈480–600 that everything sits on: a meadow edge, a tabletop, a skirting board. Put pale distant ranges or haze bands behind it, getting lighter with distance. Near ground is darker and slightly desaturated (sage `#6E9F8A`→`#C9E2D4`, or floor `#6E9DB2`→`#9CC3D3`).
- **The pun object is mid-ground and large** (≥180px), close to centre (x 500–1150). Keep it as a flat shape with a little light-and-shade modelling: one highlight strip, one shade strip, and a soft blurred ellipse beneath as a contact shadow.
- **One warm accent on the payoff**, the thing that represents the healthy choice (the bench, the sun). Use `#E8A317` with `#B97F0C` for its shade and `#F6BE4A` for a radial highlight. Stage it against a pale background so it pops at thumbnail size. Echo the warmth only as faint `#FCEBC4` light spill (a caustic pool, a floor glow). Never use a second warm object.
- **The "problem" (phone/feed) stays cool and small, or recedes.** Screens are navy/primary rounded rects with a pale dot and a dark bottom bar, and never use real app UI. Push the phone into the distance, into shadow (a dark doorway), or let it be the stream that runs away.
- **A tiny scale cue:** a faceless `#24465A` seated or standing silhouette (circle head and a rounded body, about 50–100px), or a tiny object in the far room. Use at most one.
- **Depth through atmosphere, not outlines:** haze layers and mist (Gaussian blur stdDeviation 10–22), facets as low-opacity black or white overlays, and a thin pale rim highlight on edges. Use no strokes except for glass rims and cables.
- **Process:** perspective ribbons and paths are easiest to build with a small Node generator (Catmull-Rom path, width ∝ distance below the horizon, vertical squash 0.5). Check that a falling or hanging part is not hidden behind the near surface. Check the 300px thumbnail and the 16:10 crop before calling it done. The SVG should be 8–32 KB.
