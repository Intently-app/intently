# versus family recipe (from respite-vs-opal and best-digital-wellbeing-apps-2026)

- **Grid.** Comparisons split the canvas on a steep diagonal (clip paths `L`/`R`, e.g. `0,0 1060,0 600,900 0,900`) with a 6px `#F7FBFC` seam line. The rival side is dark (ink→deep brand sky, `#0B1C27`→`#24465A`) and the Respite side is light (`#DCEEF4`→`#F7FBFC`). Roundups instead put the whole lineup in one group rotated about -11° around the canvas centre. The diagonal is the composition.
- **Horizon.** Shared ground or shelf line around y 560–640. Above it sit big flat fields and the subjects; below it is one heavy flat field (water, a dark under-shelf wall) that is left nearly empty as negative space.
- **Halo disc.** A big flat circle (r 250–300), one step lighter than its field, sits behind the focal subject: `#BDE1EC` on light grounds, `#41788F` on dark ones. A roundup adds a blurred radial glow over it. It is a single disc, never concentric rings, which read as a donut.
- **Light and hard shadows.** Light comes from the upper right. Every object casts a hard, unblurred shadow down and to the left: the same silhouette moved by `translate(-26 14)` in `#0B1C27` at .45, or a slanted parallelogram on water. Lit faces are on the right with a thin `#F7FBFC` highlight stripe inside the right edge, and a darker strip runs down the left edge.
- **Construction.** Objects are crisp polygons and rects with no outlines. Volume comes from a two-tone top face and front face (stepping stones, plank, shelf) with a small pale glint bar on top. Glass is a flat body, a darker liquid fill clipped to the silhouette, and a highlight stripe.
- **Warm accent.** Only the Respite or highlighted object gets `#E8A317`, with a `#B97F0C` side and a `#FCEBC4` glint. Its cast shadow uses `#B97F0C`. Everything else stays mist, navy, sage (`#A8CDB2`/`#6E9E7E`) or green. Any warm material in the brief (wood) is pushed dark (`#6E4A2C`/`#5E3E22`) so it never competes.
- **Scale and story.** One tiny faceless navy figure (a head circle plus a tapered body) may stand on the focal object to say "you decide here." Keep it at most about 130px tall.
- **Craft details.** Add a few sparse ripple or coursing bars (4px rects at low opacity) for texture rather than gradients. Keep every focal subject inside x 650–1300, y 300–650, so it survives the card crop.
