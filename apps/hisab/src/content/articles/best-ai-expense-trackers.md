"AI expense tracker" now covers very different products: budgeting apps with an AI connector added on, AI apps with a finance feature added on, and services built specifically for an AI to operate.

We make one of the products on this list, and it isn't open to the public yet, so it goes last. We haven't used each of the others hands-on. This comparison comes from their own public pages, and where one company's claim about another is all we could find, we say so.

## Finances in ChatGPT

**Best for:** people in the US who already use ChatGPT and want answers about linked accounts.

It reads balances, transactions, investments and liabilities through Plaid. [It can't move money](https://decrypt.co/368039/chatgpt-see-bank-account-what-actually-means). It began as a preview for US Pro users in May 2026, and [October reports](https://mixed-news.com/en/?p=35285) said it was reaching the Free plan in the US. If your spending isn't in a linked account, such as cash, it won't appear. See [what ChatGPT can do outside this feature](/articles/can-chatgpt-track-your-spending/).

## Monarch

**Best for:** households that want a full budgeting app, with AI access as an extra.

Monarch is an established bank-linked budgeting app at $99.99 a year. Era's comparison describes an official connector in beta for several AI apps. Monarch's own pricing page doesn't mention it, so check the current state before relying on it.

## Copilot Money

**Best for:** Apple users who want a polished, automatic budgeting app.

Copilot runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web, at $109 billed yearly. There is no Android app listed. Era's comparison describes its AI connector as read-only.

## YNAB

**Best for:** people who want a strict budgeting method and are willing to work at it.

YNAB is built around assigning every dollar a job. It is not an AI product, and Era's comparison says there is no official MCP server, only community-built ones.

## Era

**Best for:** people outside the US who want bank data available to their AI.

Era is built as a layer between your accounts and AI apps, and it says it connects through regulated aggregators in the US, UK, Canada, France, Germany, Spain and 40-plus countries in all. [Its free tier](https://era.app/pricing) allows manual accounts with read-only AI access. Live connections and write access start at the Organize plan, at $6.99 a month billed yearly. It is the one product here reporting agent-initiated transfers, in private beta.

## AI Money

**Best for:** phone-first users who want careful limits on what the AI sees.

An app with a connector on its Elite plan. The AI [works from a summary](https://www.ai-money.app/features/claude-chatgpt-connector/) plus your latest 20 transactions, so older questions may not be answerable. It can log entries at once, and higher-risk changes wait for your approval. Daily caps are 300 logged transactions and 30 proposals.

## MyFinance MCP

**Best for:** technical users, or anyone who wants open source and low cost.

[MyFinance MCP](https://myfinance-mcp.com/) is MIT-licensed and free during its beta, and you can host it yourself. It supports more than 150 currencies, and you log by talking to your AI, with the totals computed in SQL. It works with any bank through statement import, and direct EU and UK bank sync is in closed beta.

## Hisab

**Best for, once it opens:** people whose money moves through cash, wallets and bank texts, and who want real books.

Hisab keeps double-entry accounts and accepts entries from AI chat, Android bank and wallet texts, receipts and statements, with no bank login. Views you save stay live on the web and Android. It is in private testing on web and Android, so you can't choose it yet. iPhone comes later, and we have only finished testing with Claude so far.

## How to choose

1. **Are you in the US, with spending in linked accounts?** Start with Finances in ChatGPT.
2. **Do you want a complete budgeting app?** Look at Monarch, Copilot or YNAB.
3. **Do you want your AI to log things for you?** Rule out the read-only tools, then check that [your AI plan allows it](/articles/can-chatgpt-track-your-spending/).
4. **Is your money mostly in cash and wallets?** Bank-linked tools will miss most of it. Read [how to track bKash and Nagad spending](/articles/track-bkash-nagad-spending/).
5. **Before connecting anything,** go through [the safety checklist](/articles/is-it-safe-to-connect-ai-to-your-money/).
